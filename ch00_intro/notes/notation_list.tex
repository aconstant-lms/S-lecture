\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\sffamily\bfseries Differential operators (Cartesian components)}\\[2pt]

$\dive\vv$, $\dive\sig$ & divergence, $v_{i,i}$ and $(\dive\sig)_i=\sigma_{ij,j}$\\

$\Kad$, $\Sad$ & kinematically and statically admissible fields\\

\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\sffamily\bfseries Dynamical systems and bifurcation (Chapter~1)}\\[2pt]
$\dot y=dy/dt$ & time derivative\\
$\dot y=f(y,r)$, \ $r$ & evolution equation, control parameter\\
$y^*$ & steady state (equilibrium), $f(y^*,r)=0$\\
$f_y$, $f_r$ & partial derivatives $\partial f/\partial y$, $\partial f/\partial r$\\
$\lambda=f_y(y^*,r)$ & growth rate of small disturbances; stable if $\lambda<0$\\
$\tau=1/\abs{\lambda}$ & relaxation time of small disturbances\\
$V(y)$ & potential, $\dot y=-dV/dy$\\
$W$, $E$ & Lyapunov function; mechanical energy\\
$\dot{\mat{y}}=\mat{f}(\mat{y},r)$, $\mat{J}$ & $n$ unknowns; Jacobian $J_{ij}=\partial f_i/\partial y_j$ at a steady state\\
$\mat{J}\mat{v}=\mat{0}$, $\mat{w}\T\mat{J}=\mat{0}\T$ & zero eigenvalue: right (mode) and left eigenvectors; fold if $\mat{w}\T\mat{f}_r\neq0$\\
$z=\rho e^{i\varphi}$, $\omega$, $\ell_1$ & Hopf normal form: amplitude, frequency, first Lyapunov coefficient\\
$\mat{u}=(\mat{y},r)$, $s$, $\mat{t}$ & continuation: unknowns with the parameter, arclength, unit tangent\\
$\widehat{\mat{J}}$ & augmented Jacobian of pseudo-arclength continuation\\
$h$, $h_c$ & imperfection, its value at the folds\\
$P$, $P_c$; \ $\theta$, $\theta_0$ & load, critical load; rotation and initial tilt of the rigid bar\\[6pt]
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\sffamily\bfseries Stability of elastic systems (from Chapter~2)}\\[2pt]
$\eta$, $\eta_c$ & load parameter, its critical value ($\lambda$ is kept for growth rates, multipliers and the Lam\'e constant)\\
$\mathcal{E}(\mat{u},\eta)$ & potential energy of the system; $\mat{u}$ column of generalised coordinates\\
$\mat{K}=\mathcal{E}_{,\mat{u}\mat{u}}$, $\mat{M}$, $T$ & stiffness matrix (Hessian), mass matrix, kinetic energy\\
$\mat{Q}$, $\mat{C}$ & non-conservative generalised forces; damping matrix\\
${}^0\mat{u}(\eta)$, $(\mat{u}_c,\eta_c)$ & principal branch; critical point\\
${}^1\mat{u}$, $\xi$, ${}^2\mat{u}$ & buckling mode (unit), its amplitude, second-order field (${}^2\mat{u}\perp{}^1\mat{u}$)\\
$\eta_1$, $\eta_2$ & Koiter's coefficients, $\eta=\eta_c+\eta_1\xi+\tfrac12\eta_2\xi^2+\dots$\\
$\kappa'$, $\mathcal{E}_3$, $\mathcal{E}_4$ & loss of stiffness in the mode; cubic and quartic energy terms along the mode\\
$\bar\xi$, $\eta_m$ & imperfection amplitude (in the mode), maximum load of the imperfect system\\
$P_f$ & flutter load\\[6pt]

$E$, $\nu$, $\lambda$, $\mu$, $K$ & Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio, Lam\'e constants, bulk modulus (moduli in GPa, $\nu$ dimensionless)\\
$\bbC$, $\bbS$ & elastic moduli (GPa) and compliances (GPa$^{-1}$); Voigt components $c_{IJ}$, $s_{IJ}$\\
$\bbK$, $K_{ij}$ & tensor of diffusion coefficients and its components (e.g.\ W\,m$^{-1}$K$^{-1}$ for heat)\\
$\sY$ & initial yield stress (MPa)\\[6pt]
