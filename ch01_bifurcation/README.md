# Chapter 1: Differential equations and bifurcation

Label prefix `st` (historical; `sec:st-*`, `eq:st-*`, `fig:st-*`, `exo:st-*`, bibitem keys `st-*`).
Status: first draft (October 2026). Template chapter of the book.

## Content
Growth and decay; steady states and the slope test; the pitchfork; why bifurcations happen;
saddle-node, transcritical and pitchfork; potential energy, buckling of a rigid bar, imperfections, hysteresis.
