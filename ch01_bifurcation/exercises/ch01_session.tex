\spart{The pitchfork by hand}
Consider $\dot y = ry - y^3$.
\q Find the steady states as functions of $r$.
\begin{answer}
$y=0$ for all $r$, and $y=\pm\sqrt r$ for $r>0$.
\end{answer}
\q Decide their stability from the slope $f_y$.
\begin{answer}
$f_y = r-3y^2$. At $y=0$, $f_y=r$: stable for $r<0$, unstable for $r>0$.
At $y=\pm\sqrt r$, $f_y=-2r<0$: both are stable.
\end{answer}
