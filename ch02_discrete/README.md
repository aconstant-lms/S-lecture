## Planned content
- Lagrange's equations, conservative loads, the total potential energy and its Hessian.
- Stability by the energy criterion; critical points as zero eigenvalues of the Hessian.
- Koiter's asymptotic analysis with one and two degrees of freedom: stable and unstable symmetric points, asymmetric points, the two-thirds and one-half power laws.
- Classic models: rigid bars on springs, the von Mises truss (snap-through), the Augusti model (mode interaction), the Ziegler column (follower load, flutter).
- Exercises: symbolic post-buckling in Mathematica, imperfection-sensitivity curves in Python.
