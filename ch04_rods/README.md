## Planned content
- Kirchhoff rods: geometry, internal forces and moments, the balance equations.
- The Euler elastica: closed form with elliptic integrals, stable post-buckling.
- Beams on an elastic foundation: wavelength selection.
- Twist, writhe and naturally curved rods (towards tape springs and deployables).
- Exercises: the elastica by shooting in Python, comparison Mathematica / FEniCS / Cast3M; discrete elastic rods.
