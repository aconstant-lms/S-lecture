## Planned content
- The F\"oppl--von K\'arm\'an equations: bending against stretching.
- Linear buckling of rectangular plates; the Galerkin method.
- Stable post-buckling and the effective width.
- Wrinkling, thermal and residual stresses; localised structures (d-cones, ridges) and their scaling laws.
- Exercises: Galerkin in Mathematica, plate eigenproblem in FEniCS.
