# Chapter 8: Shells in service: nozzles, prestress and deployable structures

## Planned content
- Shells under external pressure: spheres, cones, the divergent of a rocket nozzle (side loads, flow separation).
- Precisely engineered imperfections in spherical shells.
- Prestressed and bistable shells: residual stresses as a design variable.
- Deployable structures: tape springs, snap-through, buckling-induced kirigami and metamaterials.
- Exercises: continuation of the snap-through of a deployable structure.
